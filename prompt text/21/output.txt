Done and released as v1.20 (pushed to main, so it's deploying now).

- **Tokens**: each model's token is now its maker's whole logo, cut out like a sticker with a white outline and a slight shadow, in place of the small logo in a coloured ring. Kimi's sits on the black tile of Kimi's own icon, and MiMo keeps Xiaomi's orange "mi". The rule-based bot keeps its lettered disc.
- **Houses and hotels**: they now stand on the property's colour band, as on the printed board: a green house for each house and a red hotel, each outlined in white so it reads on every colour, the green streets included. Up to three houses or a hotel take the band's full thickness and a little more, and four houses fill its length. They are much bigger than the old little triangles and the word HOTEL, and they now show on a phone too, where those were hidden. On the Mega board the skyscraper is a grey tower in the same style.

Without the ring, a token no longer shows its player's colour. If two seats ever came from the same maker, two OpenAI models say, their tokens would look the same; hovering one shows the player's name. If you'd rather, the outline can be drawn in the player's colour instead of white.
