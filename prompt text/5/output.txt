Both are done and released as v1.4 on main.

The board is always on the table now. Whenever there is no game to show, before the first game, while one loads, when a link points at a game that does not exist, or when the database is not set up yet, the full board is drawn with every square, its colour band and its price, the logo in the centre and the notice in place of the match figures, instead of the empty frame in your screenshot.

The editions. A "Board" button sits in the header on every tab; it opens a list with a search box above it. Type to narrow it, press Enter to take the first match or click one, Escape closes it. Seven editions are in: London, Atlantic City, Paris, the German classic, the Italian classic, the Dutch classic and the 2008 World Edition. Choosing one renames the squares by position and changes every amount on the page into that edition's currency: the squares and their prices, the deed card, the event feed (including the cards drawn and the names and sums inside what the players say), the player cards and their takings, the match figures in the centre, and both statistics pages. The game itself is untouched, so you can switch while watching one live and the replay carries on. The choice is kept on the device. The Madrid board stayed out: I could confirm only part of it, and a wrong street is worse than a missing city. Adding an edition is forty names in board order in one file.

Two limits worth knowing. The Mega board has its own fifty-two squares, so a Mega game keeps London names and takes only the currency. The transcripts the site produces for feeding to an AI stay in London names and pounds, since they are written by the server for everyone.

Verified before the push: the viewer in headless Chromium through every tab, the empty board on a missing game, the switch to Atlantic City with the board, feeds, cards and centre read in dollars and American names, the choice surviving a reload and reaching the statistics, the Dutch board with euros after the amount, a bot game followed live and the phone layout; the replay reducer against the engine for both editions; the engine check; the dry-run deploy; the rendered pages; and a look at the dark theme, which is what your screenshot was in.
