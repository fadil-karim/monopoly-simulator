Every name on the board is now set at one size: the largest at which the longest word on the board, measured in the board's own face, still fits its square, under a base a little smaller than before. So Bow Street no longer out-sizes Marlborough Street, the short names sit on one line, and the prices, the buildings and the pictures take their sizes from the names. One very long word sets the size for the whole board, which is why the Dutch edition runs smaller than London. Hovering any square shows its name and price large in a label on the square's inner side, and a Full screen button in the game bar (or the F key) puts the board alone across the window, framed as on the page, with Escape, F or the corner button to come back.
