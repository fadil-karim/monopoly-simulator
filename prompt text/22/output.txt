The site now opens on a homepage of games, and Monopoly has moved to a page of its own at `/monopoly/`. Released as v1.21 on `main`.

**The homepage**
- **The players** is at the top. It lists every model in the pool, each with its maker's logo as a sticker, its name and maker, the API it plays through, its model id and effort, and its Monopoly record (games, wins, win rate). A model playing right now shows "At the table".
- **The games** comes next. Monopoly shows what is on its table now and has links to watch, every game and both statistics pages. Chess, Checkers, Battleship and Connect 4 each have a card with its own picture, marked "Coming soon".
- Old links such as `/#live` or `/#game/…` still open the Monopoly page. The Monopoly page's logo and a new "← All games" tab lead back to the homepage.

**One step for you:** the list of players comes from the runner, which now tells the site its pool each time it starts. Until you `git pull` and restart the runner or dashboard on your Mac, the homepage lists the models that have already played.

**The plan** is in `PLAN.md`. Each game reuses what Monopoly proved: an engine that logs every move, a replay checked against it, the same providers, costs and runner, and a page per game. The order, one release each:
1. **Platform + Connect 4.** The site learns to hold several game types, two-player games get Elo ratings, and Connect 4 comes first because it is small and solved, so every move can be scored against perfect play.
2. **Chess,** with the full rules from chess.js and a draw after 300 plies.
3. **Checkers,** English draughts on its own engine.
4. **Battleship,** with hidden fleets, a test that no prompt leaks the other fleet, and a probability bot as a yardstick.

Rough cost for the dearest models is $0.30–1 a game for Connect 4 and $1–4 for the others.
