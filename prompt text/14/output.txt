That is four keys in `runner/.env`: ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, GEMINI_API_KEY, PERPLEXITY_API_KEY and OPENROUTER_API_KEY. The Perplexity and OpenRouter ids come from their published catalogues and changelogs, so run `--list-models perplexity` and `--list-models openrouter` once before the first real game; a seat whose id is wrong plays its decisions by the rules-based bot and the calls are logged as fallbacks.
