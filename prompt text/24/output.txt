The breakdown is in `AI.md` at the top of the repo (released as v1.23), with six diagrams that render on GitHub and real prompts from your game. The short answers:

- **Every turn?** Yes, once, before the dice. The player whose turn it is gets a "pre-roll" question (build, sell, mortgage, unmortgage, propose trades, and the Jail choice). It is asked even when there is nothing to do, and not again after doubles.
- **Every movement?** No. Rolling, moving, passing GO, rent, tax, cards and going to Jail are all automatic.
- **Otherwise only when there's a choice:** landing on an unowned property it can afford (buy), every auction (all players bid at once), each step of a trade offered to it, being spoken to at the table (reply), and owing more than it has (raise cash).
- **What they're given:** plain text only. That means the rules, the board as a 40-line text table, every player's cash, square and net worth, every property's owner and houses, the last 30 lines of table talk it could hear, and the question with its legal options. No image, no JSON file, no tools, and no memory: every question is a fresh conversation.
- **What they send back:** one JSON object with the decision, an optional line to the table and a short reason. If it's missing, malformed or breaks the rules, the rule-based bot plays that one decision.
- **In your game** that came to 158 calls in 8 rounds, about 2.8 a turn. Pre-rolls were 36% of them and replies to table talk 25%.
