The live view is rebuilt and released as v1.2 on main.

The board now sizes itself to the window height, so the whole of it is in view on a desktop, with the table talk, events and model calls beside it and the players beneath. The centre reads top to bottom as you asked: the edition, round and turn line at the top, the dice and the last event in a box at the bottom, and between them the match while nothing is hovered: goes, properties bought (with how many went at auction), trades, cash on the table, money made from the bank, money paid to the bank, money paid between players and how much of that was rent, plus a histogram of every roll from 2 to 12 with the two-dice expectation drawn over it and the mean.

Hover any property and the centre becomes its deed card: the owner, the price and its buildings or mortgage, the rent it has generated, how many times it has been landed on, what each player has earned from it, and its ledger of purchases, auctions, trades, mortgages, buildings and bankruptcy transfers, newest first. A click keeps it there after the pointer leaves, and a second click releases it.

Under the board each player's card shows their cash and net worth, their properties as colour-banded chips with the building level, what they have made in total and paid out, the split into rent, GO, other money from the bank and other money from players, the take from each of their properties, and their own roll histogram in their colour.

The colours are Monopoly's: the board green, cream squares with black rules, the red logo plate, and the official property group colours from brown to dark blue. The face is Jost, an open geometric face in the spirit of the Kabel lettering on the box, self-hosted so the site still loads nothing from elsewhere.
